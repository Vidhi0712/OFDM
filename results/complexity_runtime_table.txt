Runtime measured with time.perf_counter over 30 bursts x 10 frames (SNR 15 dB, 5 taps), median of 3 repeats;
includes each strategy's own front-end work (pilot LS, noise estimate). Environment: Python 3.13.3, NumPy 2.2.6, Windows-11-10.0.26300-SP0, AMD64 Family 25 Model 80 Stepping 0, AuthenticAMD.
Runtimes are environment dependent (single-threaded NumPy; Python-loop overhead dominates the adaptive filters).

Strategy             |   us / frame | CM / frame | SE b/s/Hz | Training 
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A: LS + ZF           |        108.6 |        176 |     1.200 | none     
B: DFT-LS + MMSE     |        123.0 |        608 |     1.200 | none     
C: LMMSE + MMSE      |         84.9 |       2959 |     1.200 | none     
D: LS-init + LMS     |        117.2 |        154 |     0.960 | 2 symbols
E: LS-init + NLMS    |        138.6 |        202 |     0.960 | 2 symbols
F: LS-init + RLS     |        174.7 |        250 |     0.960 | 2 symbols

CM = complex-multiplication-equivalent proxy per received OFDM symbol (receiver.complexity_cm_per_frame).
SE = payload bits / transmitted time samples: pilots (16/64) and CP (16/64 samples) are already excluded from
payload; D-F additionally spend 2 of every 10 OFDM symbols on known training.
A-C BER does not depend on the training symbols, so their SE is computed as if those symbols were payload.
